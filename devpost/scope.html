Correct Me

Upload a photo of your handwritten math solution. It finds the first line where your reasoning broke and tells you what went wrong, without giving you the answer.

The unique kernel

It diagnoses your solution. It doesn't re-solve the problem. It respects your approach, finds the first line where it breaks, names the mistake, and asks a hint question. It never shows the corrected line or the final answer.

"They re-solve the problem and hand me their solution. I want a diagnosis of mine."

The core loopUpload once, then confirm, check, and fix until it's clean.

What "working" looks likeThe one-minute demo, with a mistake planted on line 5. Step through it.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the app lands on exactly the planted line and explains it without giving the solution away. Viewers can see the verdict is right. Step ③ shows line 6 being flagged next: the app checks each line against the one before it, not against a known answer.

Who it's for, and why it matters

Students practicing math

Algebra, differentiation, integration, short derivations. Today, when an answer is wrong, they recheck from the top again and again and test examples. Sometimes every example works, "and that teaches me nothing."

The personal stake

"An app that respects my idea, and even better, understands it, but still tells me where my reasoning broke."

Feel

"A teacher marking my notebook with a red pen, but clean and minimal."

The boundaryOpen each list.

Now: the proof of concept 5 items
  • One photo of a handwritten math solution
  • Transcription into numbered, editable lines, confirmed before checking
  • A check that finds the first wrong line: ticks before it, a red cross on it with the kind of mistake and a hint question, "not checked" after it
  • A clear "all correct" result
  • Re-checking after editing a line in the app
Later: if the math version works 4 items
  • Coding solutions — the mistake is often an idea spread over several lines
  • Re-uploading a corrected photo
  • More hints when the student is still stuck
  • History of past checks, to see which kinds of mistakes come up most
Cut 7 items
  • Showing the correct solution or final answer — permanently, because it defeats the kernel
  • Accounts and login — not needed to prove the diagnosis
  • Saved history — moved to Later
  • Typing a solution from scratch — the photo is the real workflow, and editing covers fixes
  • Multiple photos or pages, PDF upload — one photo of 5–20 lines proves it
  • Mobile app — a web page is enough for the demo
  • Coding problems in this version — math's "line N follows from line N−1" is checkable first
