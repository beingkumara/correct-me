Your product decisions 16
  • "Try a sample" is Later so build time goes to the core path and unsure behavior.
  • Name: Correct Me.
  • Line 1 is the question. No separate field; type it into line 1 if the photo lacks it.
  • The photo stays beside the lines so misreads are easy to spot.
  • Marks persist while editing "they'd lose the hint that helped them."
  • An edit fades only the lines below it. The ticks above stay valid.
  • Any line is editable, because the mistake or a misread may come earlier.
  • Check again re-judges from the top, which is why line 6 gets flagged next.
  • Printed math for the student, red handwriting for the teacher.
  • Honesty over confidence: "A confident wrong verdict is worse than an honest 'I'm not sure.'"
  • Checking continues past an unsure line and later verdicts state the assumption.
  • No lost work on failure.
  • Check waits for every "?" line to be fixed or confirmed. A misread is never blamed on the student. (Build)
  • A confirmed "?" line can't get a cross: "wrong" there shows as unsure, "I might have misread this line." (Build)
  • Unsure is decided in code: low confidence, or "right under another reading of the same notation" (sin x² as (sin x)²), becomes unsure. A different value or sign never counts as another reading. (Build)
  • A photo the reader can't make sense of gets "This doesn't look like math working I can read" in seconds, never a network message. (Final review)
What we're building 7
  • One-page app: start screen, upload, reading animation, two-column working view (stacked on narrow screens)
  • Transcription into numbered, editable, printed-math lines; line 1 as the question; "?" on unclear reads
  • First-wrong-line check: ticks, one red cross with a mistake note and a hint, "not checked", "No mistakes found."
  • Unsure verdicts: carry-on rule, assumption notes, caveated all-clear
  • Editing: "edited" and "needs re-check" marks, Check again
  • Unopenable-file, unreadable, not-math, partly readable, and failure states, with no lost work
  • "Upload a different photo"
Deferred and later 7
  • Coding solutions — the mistake is often an idea spread over lines
  • Re-uploading a corrected photo — fixes happen in the edit boxes for now
  • More hints when stuck — one hint per flagged line for now
  • History / common-mistake patterns — needs saved data, which was cut
  • Multiple photos, PDF upload — one photo proves it
  • "Try a sample" link — Later: build time goes to the core path + unsure behavior
  • Dedicated mobile app
Non-goals 5
  • Never show the corrected line, a step's right value, or the final answer. Permanent; it's the kernel.
  • No accounts or login
  • No typing a solution from scratch
  • No confident verdicts the app doesn't actually have
  • No subjects beyond math in this version

What the build changedEach came from evidence: a real run, a test, or your review.

  • Misreads aren't the student's fault. On the first real photo the reader read ∫cos as f cos, flagged it "?", and the checker still crossed the line. Now Check stays disabled until each "?" line is fixed or confirmed, and a cross on a confirmed "?" line becomes unsure.
  • "Unsure" needed code, not a request. Asking for it never produced one. On y = sin x² → dy/dx = 2 sin x cos x the checker said wrong with 0.96 confidence. The checker now reports a confidence and any alternative reading of the same notation, and the code turns low-confidence or right-under-another-reading verdicts into unsure.
  • The demo photo is six lines with the sign error on line 5, as planned: line 5 flagged, then line 6, then "No mistakes found." The squashed u = x dv = … read is kept on purpose to show the confirm step.
  • Accepted limit: the leak guard catches copied answers, not reworded near-answers like "What is x·sin x − (−cos x) equal to?"
  • Stuck reads end fast and honestly. A non-math photo or a looping reader gets the not-math note within seconds, and read failures name the photo reader, not "the checker".
